Kwacha Track

Your mobile money spending in one place: withdrawn, received, sent and total used.

Install the Android app

The app reads your Airtel, MTN and Zamtel messages by itself and keeps the same figures as the web version.

Download the app
  1. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow installs from your browser.
  2. If Play Protect warns you, tap More details, then Install anyway.
  3. Open the app, create your account and allow SMS access.

Can't install it, or it won't work?

The web version works on any phone or computer. You add your messages by pasting them, and your figures match the app when you use the same phone number and PIN.
